# Reading-method follow-up

Date: 2026-09-30.

This source review follows the [limited source selection](decipherist-reading-method-check-2026-09-30.md#additional-source-selection).
It examines material that the earlier selection left unread.
The project still has no validated Voynich key or translation.

## Ulyanenkov

The [version 10 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.04149v10) contains a printed codebook and connected proposed readings.
Pages 18–24 give entries for the main text. Pages 70–73 add entries for the last manuscript page.
Some operations are explicit: pages 25 and 77 merge identical letters at the boundaries of adjacent symbol readings.
The paper also gives exceptions and alternative values.

Selection rules remain incomplete. Figure 68 changes a repeated literal reading through an interpretation of the proposed meaning.
Figure 73 lists three possible readings and calls for further investigation.
Page 29 revises the preceding *teapot* example through a reversal rule. This audit preserves that correction.

The f11v and f75v examples provide connected proposed text, reconstructed phrases, and possible meanings on pages 47–48.
The examined method sections do not independently verify these meanings or fix all reading selections.
The final discussion retains steps that depend on interpretation on pages 76–78.

The later pages supply the codebook left unread in the earlier review.
A new reading test still needs fixed selection and interpretation rules.
This review does not evaluate the authorship and dating arguments or reject English as a possible source language.

## Jama

The [version 1 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02261v1) proposes a symbolic interpretation of page structure.
Its instructions use Fibonacci groups, prime-number groups, and the golden ratio.
Section 1 directs readers to decode selected glyphs but does not define the conversion or print the example's resulting message.
The later role descriptions do not supply that missing conversion.

The validation claims also have incorrect source references.
Page 21 reports two private readers' observations on `f91r`; page 22 describes eleven rows on that side.
Page 25 describes `f91v` as another supporting example.

[Yale's detailed catalogue](https://pre1600ms.beinecke.library.yale.edu/docs/pre1600.ms408.htm) lists folio 91 as missing.
Its [current image manifest](https://collections.library.yale.edu/manifests/2002046) contains 213 canvases and no label for either side of folio 91.
The labels go from `90v (part)` to `93r`.
The paper gives no alternative foliation that resolves these references.
Thus, these observations cannot be reproduced as cited. This audit selects no replacement folio.

The source does not support a new reading test with fixed meanings.
This result does not evaluate every structural claim or identify the cause of the incorrect references.
