# Brumbaugh source review

Date: 2026-09-30.

The project still has no validated Voynich key or translation.
[Yale's detailed catalogue](https://pre1600ms.beinecke.library.yale.edu/docs/pre1600.ms408.htm) cites three Brumbaugh papers from 1974–1976.
This review follows those references to their source material.
A catalogue citation does not independently validate a reading.

## The 1974 preview

The [publisher record](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/2851756) identifies a three-page article in *Speculum* 49, pages 546–548.
The public preview supplies page 546. Pages 547–548 remain unread.

On page 546, Brumbaugh describes a letter box with numbered columns.
Each cipher symbol can represent three plaintext letters. A second cipher gives alternative designs for each numeral.
The author selects `ARABYCCUS` from the printed matrix through context.
He also proposes artificial pseudo-Latin and phonetic spelling changes.

The preview directs readers to a later *Yale University Library Gazette* article for detailed results.
It supplies part of a method, but it cannot establish the complete article's selection rules or validation evidence.

## Later papers

The 1975 *Yale University Library Gazette* paper remains unread.
The limited search included an incorrect expansion of the journal name to *The Mathematical Gazette*.
The source record preserves that error. This search does not settle the paper's availability.

The [1976 publisher record](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.2307/751135) identifies an article on star maps, pages 139–150.
Its public preview supplies page 139. Pages 140–150 remain unread.
The preview distinguishes the last-leaf cipher from the main text cipher.
Footnote 7 describes a letter shift and anagram for a proposed name reading.
This page does not give the complete star-map reading procedure.
