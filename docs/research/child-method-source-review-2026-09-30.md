Date: 2026-09-30.

The project still has no validated Voynich key or translation.

The web reader supplied text for all four pages. Direct downloads failed, and screenshot requests supplied no usable images.
The review therefore leaves the printed glyph examples unverified.

## Method statements

Child proposes a previously unknown medieval North Germanic dialect.
PDF page 2 describes a search for connecting words, followed by proposed names and verb forms.
He starts with folio 114r and uses folio 40v as a further example.

Across PDF pages 2–3, Child says that the trial-and-error process was too long to describe in the paper.
PDF page 3 states that he has not recovered all letters.
It gives tentative phrase readings but withholds a translation of two proposed clauses.

These statements describe an incomplete proposal. They do not establish a complete method for a new reading test.
This review does not reject North Germanic languages or assess Child's later work.
