# Research pause record

Date: 2026-09-17. Research is paused at the owner's request.

## Outcome

The manuscript remains unsolved.
The project has no validated key, language identification, or translation.
It has not established the meaning of a single label.

The work produced useful constraints and negative results.
They narrow specific explanations and identify methods that do not support a reading.
They do not prove that the manuscript is meaningless or that every language or cipher model fails.
We make no claim that these observations are the first of their kind.

## Main findings

Some neighboring word parts predict each other in both transcriptions, under the specified controls.
This confirms measurable structure in the selected data.
It does not identify sounds or meaning, and the same fixed model loses prediction quality across physical line breaks.
The [boundary report](../../reports/BOUNDARY_PREDICTION.md) and [line-break report](../../reports/CROSS_LINE_TRANSFER.md) give the limits.

Two complete labels on f69v have the same recorded spelling in both transcriptions.
They cannot represent different names through one fixed function that ignores position and context.
This stops one proposed one-to-one mapping to a fixed list of 28 names.
The result remains conditional on transcription equality and label role.
The [anchor audit](anchor-audit.md#lunar-mansion-names-complete-label-collision) records the proof and image uncertainty.

The [fixed-expansion experiment](../../reports/LINE_TRIPLET_BOUNDARIES.md) constrains one three-digit encoding model.
Under its assumptions, each represented sign unit must expand to a multiple of three digits.
This does not recover digit values or plaintext.

A [text-column association](../../reports/COLUMN_ASSOCIATION.md) does not pass the additional control for physical line order.
We stopped that statistic without further adjustment.
It does not establish copying between neighboring lines.

The latest [zodiac control](../../reports/ZODIAC_PANEL_CONTROL.md) tests a published degree alignment after preserving class counts within each physical panel.
About 42% of allowed rotations match at least as well as the published alignment.
We stopped its use as a fine positional guide to label meanings.
The result concerns the published attribute sequence; missing raw inputs prevent independent verification of its joins to images.

## Other work and its value

The project kept source hashes, separate transcription records, and reproducible experiment records.
Known-plaintext controls exposed failures and ambiguities in the search methods.
One revised Latin control eventually recovered its complete artificial test text.
That success concerned a known artificial cipher, not the Voynich manuscript.

Reviews of published reading proposals found incomplete rules, interpretation-dependent choices, or word lookup that cannot validate assigned meanings.
Several possible picture-to-name connections lacked enough independent evidence for a text test.
These are useful stop decisions. They are not translations or general disproofs of the proposed languages.

Software, test counts, reports, and publications are supporting work.
They do not count as progress toward decipherment.
The principal research value is a clearer record of which specific claims survive which tests.

## Retained work

Completed reports and aggregate results remain in the public repository.
The local ignored directories retain source captures, private inputs, experiment plans, and detailed verification records.
The public repository excludes full manuscript transcriptions, reference corpora, and private contact details.

The cyclic quotient development files are retained as an unfinished, stopped draft.
They include the design, implementation, synthetic tests, and review findings.
They have no final input freeze and produced no reference-corpus fit or manuscript result.
The [draft status](../../experiments/cyclic_quotient/README.md) lists the remaining limits.
Their preservation does not authorize execution or establish that the runner is ready.

## Resumption

The owner must ask to resume before research continues.
The existing [status record](../../STATUS.md) and [validation protocol](validation-protocol.md) remain the starting references.

A new experiment needs a stated distinction between competing explanations and evidence that the test can resolve it.
