# Cross-boundary unit prediction

## What changed

Measured prediction of the first unit after a recorded word boundary from the preceding final unit.
The [report](../../../reports/BOUNDARY_PREDICTION.md) contains the scores and controls.
The model uses the existing paragraph-line filter, physical-group split, and two unitizations.
It does not detect spaces or establish linguistic word boundaries.

## Why

Whole-word repeat and edit prediction supplied no useful order-specific gain.
This experiment tests whether a smaller relation transfers across page groups.
The conditional model reduces initial-unit loss in both transcriptions.
The result remains above three specified shuffle controls, including matched folio, position, and length strata.
The matched predecessor null can change labels for only 30.3% of ZL targets and 37.4% of IT targets.
The main unitization supplies no gain when both word types were absent from training.
This small subset limits a claim of general transfer to new words.
It does not assign sounds, meanings, or a historical writing process.

A later control holds the exact target remainder fixed within folio, position, and preceding-length cells.
The visual track has only 99 ZL and 153 IT targets in cells with both endpoints variable.
Its excess is positive. Raw-character results are weaker.
Exact expectation components identify positive `a/o` contributions and no positive `ch/sh` contribution in either transcription.
These selected cells identify image-review candidates. They do not justify sign mergers or phonetic values.

The full selected `a/o` set contains only two-unit forms with `r/l` remainders.
The sources have opposite initial readings at two f85r1 locations.
AI image readers also disagree at f86v5.35. No transcription or score was changed.
Further tuning is stopped. This branch does not identify a mechanism.

A later [cross-line comparison](../../../reports/CROSS_LINE_TRANSFER.md) tests transfer of the fixed interior endpoint table.
It uses consecutive source loci inside marked paragraphs, before line filtering.
The alternative model gives matched predecessor arrangements equal probability.
The visual comparison includes 24 informative ZL pairs and 233 IT pairs.
The conditional log2 ratios are -7.262 and -39.475, respectively.
Both results favor the equal-probability model, including after removal of any one physical group.
Raw-unit results have the same direction.
This comparison does not support use of the unchanged table across line breaks.
The result does not establish a reset mechanism or a plaintext reading.
This branch is stopped without new filters or parameters.
