# Homophonic search development

## What changed

Added a pivot-group upper bound and exact lexical local search.
Added a fixed development protocol for the inspected Italian control.
Added a supervisor for elapsed time, sampled resident memory, and completion status.

The [protocol](../../../docs/plans/homophonic-search-development-v1.md) fixes one root query and two starting maps.
The [experiment guide](../../../experiments/search_development/README.md) defines the supervised entry point.

## Why

The previous Italian searches stopped with a large optimization gap.
The group bound requires words in each group to agree on one pivot assignment.
The local search improves the same dictionary objective used by the exact solver.
These methods test finite optimization behavior. They do not establish a historical cipher.
