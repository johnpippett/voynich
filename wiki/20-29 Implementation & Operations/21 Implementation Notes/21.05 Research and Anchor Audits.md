## Why

These records separate structural claims from plaintext recovery.
A reversed n-gram score can reflect boundaries and estimation rules. It does not establish physical reading direction by itself.
Repeated glyph strings constrain plaintext equality only under a declared context-independent or same-state decoder.
The released Antidotarium text mixes source languages and editorial material. It is not accepted as a clean Latin reference.

## Verification

The audit uses versioned author records, primary catalogues, and source-code inspection.
The directionality review confirms the reversal identities and the finite boundary counterexample.
Notebook metadata does not establish the requested historical version. The audit keeps that limit explicit.
The Antidotarium archive matched its declared size and checksum. No downloaded code was executed.
These records do not establish a manuscript key, language, or translation.

## Rollback

Correct a disputed source claim in a new commit. Retain the cited version and the reason for correction.
Do not replace uncertainty with a guessed reading.
